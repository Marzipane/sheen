# Navigation

`SheenTabBar` is the floating tab bar of iOS 26, with a lens that follows your finger; `SheenTopTabBar` is its form
for wide windows. `SheenToolbar` floats over content; `SheenActionBar` holds a total and its action;
`SheenStepsLine` shows where a flow is.
