# Sheets

`showSheenSheet` opens a sheet with two heights, a grabber and a header; `showSheenCustomSheet` lets you draw the
whole surface. `showSheenDateSheet` picks a date on wheels, `showSheenDocumentSheet` shows long text, and
`SheenFormSheet` turns a page into a centred card on tablets.
